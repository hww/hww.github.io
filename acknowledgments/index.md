## Gary Hybatko

Principal R&D Engineer, Software Architect, and Systems Programmer

A brilliant analytical programmer possessing a truly encyclopedic mind. Throughout his career, Gary has mastered and solved some of the most complex low-level engineering challenges across multiple domains, ranging from circuit board design and FPGA/ASIC development to deep embedded and system-level software architecture.
Key Highlights:

* Linux Pioneer: A hardcore developer and a long-time power user of Gentoo Linux.
* Hardware Magic: Developed the famous Karat Instant Replay cartridge for Nintendo game systems—a highly advanced cheating device that became a major commercial success in Japan. He was also one of the lead developers behind one of the world's very first hardware 3D graphics accelerators.
* Technical Leadership: Served as the trusted Chief Technology Officer (CTO) for our core engineering group for many years, driving innovation and architecture for SyberSoft (Riga), Intrec Consulting (Cyprus), Digital Chile (USA), Game Tools (USA), and Rocket Amusements (USA).

Gary is a remarkably kind soul and an incredibly fascinating human being. He is one of those rare, brilliant hidden gems who might not seek the spotlight, but once you know him, he reveals himself as a true engineering treasure. He is my friend, my mentor, and my teacher.

## David Siller

Director, Producer, and Legendary Video Game Designer

David is a seminal video game director, producer, and designer whose roots trace back to the dawn of modern gaming in 1985. Over his illustrious career, he has shaped projects and collaborated with giants like Sony Interactive Entertainment, Capcom, SEGA, and Naughty Dog.
Key Projects and Legacy:

* Industry Roots: Contributed to the launch of Capcom's legendary Ghosts 'n Goblins (1985).
* Design & Direction: Served as the Director for Taz-Mania and spearheaded the production of Aero the Acro-Bat 2 (developed by Acclaim Studios Austin).
* The Crash Bandicoot Era: Played a foundational role as a Producer in shaping the original Crash Bandicoot universe, including the Hog Wild project. He famously and uncreditably lent his own voice to the iconic character Aku Aku.
* Later Milestones: Directed the critically acclaimed action-adventure title Maximo: Ghosts to Glory for Capcom.

David is a beautiful human being and a deeply loving husband. He possesses an incredibly gifted, emotional, and artistic soul, with a lifelong affection for scale-modeling historic racing cars. He is my dear friend, my guide, and my mentor.
